# Business Logic & Evaluation Framework

The **AI Evaluation QA Framework** is built on a foundation of rigorous evaluation logic designed to provide objective, actionable insights into LLM performance.

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Scoring Rubric
Responses are evaluated across four key criteria:
- **Accuracy**: Factual correctness and alignment with expected answers.
- **Reasoning**: Quality of logical flow and structured thinking.
- **Tone**: Appropriateness of voice and professionalism.
- **Completeness**: Coverage of all relevant aspects and response depth.

## 2. Automated Scoring Heuristics
The framework uses deterministic rules to provide consistent scoring:
- **Marker Detection**: Identification of uncertainty markers, logical connectors, and politeness indicators.
- **Pattern Matching**: Recognition of evidence attribution and redundancy.
- **Length Analysis**: Context-aware evaluation of response depth.

## 3. Defect Detection System
Automated identification of common LLM failure modes:
- **Hallucinations**: Factual inconsistencies.
- **Logical Flaws**: Reasoning gaps or contradictions.
- **Redundancy**: Repetitive content patterns.
- **Incompleteness**: Truncated or insufficient responses.

## 4. Cost and Performance Telemetry
- **Token Tracking**: Precision counting for accurate cost estimation.
- **Execution Analytics**: Monitoring of response times and success rates.
- **Regression Detection**: Statistical comparison against baselines to ensure continuous quality.

## 5. Decision Support
The framework transforms raw model outputs into interactive dashboards and executive summaries, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions about model selection and prompt engineering.
